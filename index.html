How it works

You talk. Your Claude thinks. Orrin speaks and shows.

Orrin isn’t another AI. It’s the wiring — ears, a mouth, and a screen — around the Claude you already have.

1

Say “Otto.”

Wake it hands-free with your word, or click the mic. No cloud round-trip to start — the mic is transcribed right on your Mac.

2

It runs your Claude

Orrin drives the Claude Code on your machine — your connectors, subagents, skills, and the project you’re working in. Nothing to reconfigure.

3

It speaks and shows

You hear the answer and watch it land on a live dashboard — real weather, your GitHub activity, charts and diagrams. The full transcript waits in a drawer.

What you get

A face for the Claude you already trust.

Inherits your whole setup

Gmail, Drive, GitHub, your files — every MCP connector, subagent, and skill in your Claude, now by voice. Because Orrin loads your config.

Fast, on-device speech

Transcription runs on the Apple-silicon GPU (MLX Whisper, large-v3-turbo) and Orrin starts talking at the first sentence — a conversation, not a round-trip. Understands any language, including Hinglish.

Knows your voice

Enroll once by reading a few phrases. Orrin answers you and ignores the TV, the room, and its own echo — register more than one voice for a shared Mac.

A teammate while you’re away

Opt-in: when you’re idle it researches your projects and — when you turn it on — opens ready-for-review pull requests, never touching main itself. A steward keeps the Mac tidy. Off by default; you merge everything.

A live dashboard + quiet HUD

Weather, air quality, GitHub activity, charts — answers land as widgets that appear only when there’s data. A small status pill tucks into the notch and auto-hides when idle.

Remembers across sessions

A private, per-project memory (plus a global tier about you) that every session reads and writes — it learns what’s worth keeping on its own, so continuity just happens.

Hands-free, in your menu bar

A state-colored icon — idle, listening, thinking, speaking — a project and chat switcher, and a self-healing mic that tells you aloud if it ever drops, instead of going silent.

Private by design

Speech, speaker-ID and memory all run on your Mac. No new account, no telemetry, no Orrin cloud in the middle — only your prompt reaches your Claude.

Also an MCP for Claude

Register Orrin once and any Claude session gains a voice and a screen — speak, ask, show.
