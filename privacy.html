Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This policy explains what data Orrin (by dockndevai) collects and how it is used. In short: the Orrin app runs on your Mac and does not send your content to us.

The app is local by design

Your microphone audio is transcribed on your device using a local speech model. Only the resulting text prompt is sent to your own Claude — exactly as if you had typed it. Orrin does not operate a server that receives, stores, or processes your conversations, audio, or files. What Claude does with your prompts is governed by Anthropic’s privacy terms.

Purchases

When you buy Orrin, checkout is handled by our merchant of record, Lemon Squeezy (payments via Stripe). They collect the information needed to process your order (name, email, billing/payment details) under their own privacy policy. We do not receive or store your card details. We receive order and license information (such as your email and purchase) to deliver your license key and provide support.

Website

This website is a static informational page. We do not run advertising or sell your data.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of the limited order data we hold by emailing us. We retain order records as required for tax and accounting purposes.
