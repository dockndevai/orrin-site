# Orrin — a voice & a screen for the Claude you already run

**Website: [dockndevai.github.io/orrin-site](https://dockndevai.github.io/orrin-site/)**

**Orrin** is a macOS app that gives **Claude Code** — the AI coding tool you already run on
your Mac — a **voice** and a **JARVIS-style on-screen assistant**. Talk to your own Claude,
hear answers out loud, and watch them render as live visuals (a clock, a weather scene, a
diagram, a chart) on a canvas.

- 🎙️ **Talk to your own Claude** — voice in, voice out
- 🖥️ **Canvas-first answers** — most replies appear as a visual, not a wall of text
- 🧩 **Inherits your setup** — your MCP connectors, subagents, skills & `CLAUDE.md`
- 🍎 **Lives in your menu bar** — starts at login
- 🔒 **Private by design** — audio is transcribed on-device; only your prompt reaches Claude
- 🔌 **Also an MCP for Claude** — any Claude session gains a voice and a screen

**macOS 12+ · Apple silicon · Bring your own Claude · one-time purchase.**
